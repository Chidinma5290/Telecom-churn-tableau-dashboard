
# 📊 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis - Tableau Dashboard

By Chidinma |  Data Analyst

###  About This Project
As part of my data analysis journey, I wanted to understand why a telecom company is losing customers. I built this interactive dashboard in Tableau to turn raw customer data into clear business insights.

###  What I Analyzed
I worked with a telecom customer dataset with over 7,000 customers to answer:
- What is our overall churn rate?
- Which customers are most likely to leave?
- What factors drive churn?

###  What I Did
1. Data Preparation:  Cleaned and prepared telecom data for analysis (Tenure, Contract, Monthly Charges, Total Charges, Services, Churn status)
2. **Built KPIs in Tableau:** Calculated Overall Churn Rate (26.5%), Average Charges for Churned vs Retained customers
3. **Created Visuals:** 
   - Map: Churn by State
   - Bar Charts: Churn by Contract Type, Tenure Group, Monthly Charge Group, Services
   - KPI Cards for quick business overview
4. **Designed Interactive Dashboard:** Combined all visuals into one interactive dashboard with filters for stakeholder exploration

###  My Key Findings
From my analysis, I discovered:

**1. Revenue is at Risk:** Customers who churned paid MORE on average ($199.30) than those who stayed ($163.01). We are losing high-value customers.

**2. Contract is the #1 Driver:** 
- Month-to-Month contracts: 37.3% churned - they have no commitment
- Two-Year contracts: Only 12.7% churned - commitment keeps them

**3. First Year is Critical:** Customers with 0-12 months tenure have 50.6% churn rate. Onboarding in the first year is failing.

**4. High Monthly Bills Push People Away:** Customers paying $200+ monthly have 63.3% churn. Pricing is a pain point.

###  My Recommendation
If I were advising this company, I would suggest:
- Offer incentives to move Month-to-Month customers to One-Year/Two-Year contracts
- Create a special retention program for the first 12 months
- Review pricing strategy for customers with monthly charges > $100
